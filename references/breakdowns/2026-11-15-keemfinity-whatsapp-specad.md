## Extracted style
- **Color:** light brand world, not dark keynote: stage #EFEAE2, brand green #25D366, deep #1DAA61, ticks #53BDEB, bubble #D9FDD3, text #111B21 / #667781.
- **Type:** Inter + Inter Display 400–700; text holds still when it must be read.
- **Motion:** eased Bézier heads; ease-back pops; decaying shakes; motion blur (4 sub-frames) only on fast moves.
- **Finishing:** wallpaper drift ~10%, key light, mint glow 6%, bloom ≤6%, grain 3%, warm vignette 16%.
- **Sound:** 95 cues; music ~95 BPM cut on its own downbeats; VO at −14 LUFS master, voice ≥12 dB over music in 1–4 kHz.
