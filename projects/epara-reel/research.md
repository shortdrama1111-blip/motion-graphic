# epara — research (https://epara.app/sq, 2026-11-15)

Site is behind an Azure WAF that blocks some automated requests; retrying with a normal browser UA gets through.

## What it is (from the site + user)
Digital wallet / financial institution, no branches — everything in the app. Tagline: **"epara, Më mirë se para!"**
User adds: top-up at any ATM, virtual + physical card, QR payments, send & receive money, credit, RIA transfers, no bank branch needed.

## Claims on the site (verbatim facts, Albanian)
- Become a user in minutes, any time, free, **pa shkuar në degë** — just the phone.
- Send money to other epara users **menjëherë, falas, 24/7**.
- **Mbi 140 ATM-të e BKT-së** in Albania: add money and withdraw cash any time.
- **Kartë virtuale** instantly for safe online shopping.
- **Kartë fizike**, usable everywhere, also abroad, pays in local currency.
- Accounts in **LEK dhe EUR**.
- Add money from a bank account, debit or credit card.
- Utility bills (energji, ujë, telefon) any time.
- **Cashback / shpërblime**.
- **Transfertat RIA**; epara Biznes; QR payments, accepted in **mijëra dyqane, restorante, bare, kafene, hotele**.
- Site device: every question answered with a big **"PO"** (YES).
- Credit (kredi): from the user; not on the homepage — confirm wording before claiming.
