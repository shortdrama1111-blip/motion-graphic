# Screen → World (fly-through)
- **Category:** transition / camera · **Engine:** three.js / 2.5D · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z]

After the action in the app (tap), the line shoots out and the camera flies through the phone screen; on the drop the screen content becomes a 3D world seen from above (e.g. a night city), blocks rise, the camera swoops to a 3/4 view and the line draws a route through the streets to a pin, while a glass UI card (status + 4-step tracker) floats over it. Exit with a dive into defocus.
