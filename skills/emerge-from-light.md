# Emerge From the Light
- **Category:** transition · **Engine:** three.js / canvas · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-moniepoint-listen]

Light swells and fades on the tap/beat; the first object starts tiny **on the camera ray through the light, at its own final depth**, and grows to size (settles ~1 s). Floor rings spread. Sound: the brand's chime + tight transient + UI pop.
