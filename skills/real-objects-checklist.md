# Real-Objects Checklist
- **Category:** QA / craft · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z], [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-moniepoint-listen]

Before approving any object or UI, check that:
- objects have bevelled edges, a little thickness and material detail (tape on boxes, soft dents, card edge shine);
- shadows grow and sharpen as objects land and touch on the exact frame;
- UI shows real rows (autocomplete, prices, names), not skeleton bars;
- people are real where people matter (footage beats drawn silhouettes);
- hand-overs never show two carrier heads, and there's no stepped motion blur or clipped text in 9:16. Check frame by frame and on a phone.
