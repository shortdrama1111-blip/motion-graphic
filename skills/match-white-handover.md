# Push Into Face → Page (invisible hand-over)
- **Category:** transition · **Engine:** three.js · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-moniepoint-listen]

Camera pushes (log-space) into a card's face while its content fades. When the blank face fills frame, swap to a new plane/page whose white is matched **to the pixel**. The next scene (e.g. the closing line) is written on that page.
