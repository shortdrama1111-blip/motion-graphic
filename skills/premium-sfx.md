# Premium SFX (not cheap)
- **Category:** audio · **Code:** `projects/epara-lights-on/audio/mix.py`

User feedback: UI pops, dings, counters and app jingles sound cheap. Use few, designed sounds: band-limited **air** swells on real moves, **sub** hits on impacts/drops, glassy **shimmer** pads (slow attack) for magic moments, quiet granular **sparkle**, a long low-passed wind for flights, a riser cut 70 ms before the drop — all through a small **room reverb**. Mix SFX well under the voice (≈ −23 LUFS pre-master).
