# Cards Off Screen (3D fan)
- **Category:** UI / depth · **Engine:** CSS 3D / three.js · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z]

Search results (≈6 cards) lift off the phone screen into real 3D depth (translateZ toward camera, slight rotateY per card), fan out with a stagger and settle; the carrier line traces the outline of the chosen card, then the camera pushes into it. Cards carry real content (photo, title, price, stars), never placeholder bars.
