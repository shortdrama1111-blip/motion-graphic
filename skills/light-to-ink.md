# Light → Ink
- **Category:** transition / brand · **Engine:** any · **Source:** [ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z]

The carrier light wraps an object once, lands on its face in the shape of the brand mark, and over ~0.5 s cools from emissive (glow, bloom, bright) into flat printed ink (matte colour with a slight multiply into the material, e.g. kraft cardboard). Drive both from one progress value: glow alpha 1→0, ink alpha 0→1, colour from hot (lighter, saturated) to the printed tone.
