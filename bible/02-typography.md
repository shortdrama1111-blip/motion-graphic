## Rules from references
- Text holds still whenever it has to be read. [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-whatsapp-specad]
- **Rise in:** words rise through a mask; alpha fades in over the first 35% of the rise so ascender tips never show as loose dots. Letter stagger ~35 ms. [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-moniepoint-listen]
- **Exit:** blur 0→7px + tracking −2→+4 and fade *in place*. Never sink or slice a word out. [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-moniepoint-listen]
- Hero words big and alone (e.g. "Always" 170px/700, no full stop). Small tracked caps for labels ("MADE BY" 15px).
