# Preset — Kinetic Type
[ref: 2026-11-15-starter-kit-prompts]

- Text is the whole video. No cards, no boxes.
- Font: Archivo Black (`assets/fonts/ArchivoBlack-400.woff2`), ALL CAPS, huge — main word fills most of the width.
- Colours: black bg, white text, one accent **#c6ff3d** for the most important word per screen.
- Motion: words slam in one by one, fast and snappy (power4.out / cubic-bezier(0.165,0.84,0.44,1), 0.25–0.4 s). Mix: slide from side, stretch from squashed to full height, split letters apart and back. Each screen exits fast before the next hits.
- Cut screens on the rhythm of the words, like a beat.
