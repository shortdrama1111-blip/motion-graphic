# Preset — Pop Bold
[ref: 2026-11-15-starter-kit-prompts]

- Loud, playful, flat colour. No gradients, no glass.
- Font: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (`assets/fonts/BricolageGrotesque-800.woff2`), big and chunky.
- Colours: flat **#c6ff3d** lime, **#8a3cff** purple, **#ff4fd8** pink, **#111111**; 4px black outlines; hard 8px offset shadows, no blur.
- Extras: rotating star/circle stickers, little arrows, simple CSS shapes.
- Motion: bouncy/springy — back.out(2.5) pops, overshoot, wobble, squash & stretch. Stickers spin slowly the whole time.
