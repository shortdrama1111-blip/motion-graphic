# Preset — Liquid Glass
[ref: 2026-11-15-starter-kit-prompts]

- Frosted cards: white ~80% opacity, 30px backdrop blur, thin white border, 32px radius, soft shadow.
- Background: soft light gradient with 2–3 blurred colour blobs (pale blue, lilac, peach) drifting slowly.
- Font: Inter (`assets/fonts/Inter-700.woff2` headings, `Inter-400.woff2` small). Text #1d1d1f, small uppercase grey labels above headings.
- Accent: green **#34c759**, only for ticks/checks.
- Motion: calm. Cards rise 30px + fade, power3.out 0.6 s, scale 0.96→1. Text arrives piece by piece. Nothing bounces.
