# Preset — Dark Hero Stage
[ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z]

- **Stage:** near-black (#070707) with a dim warm glow (amber in the reference) that drifts and breathes; a faint brand-colour corner; fine grain.
- **Heroes bright:** phone, product/box and type are big and well lit, so the dark never feels empty.
- **One accent colour** (the brand's), used for the carrier line and at most one word per line of type.
- **UI:** the brand's app rebuilt by eye in **dark mode**, with real content (real autocomplete rows, real product cards), never skeleton bars.
- **Type:** a bold grotesk (SF Pro Display Bold in the reference); big, re-wrapped (not cropped) for 9:16.
- **Camera:** always drifting, long eases, shallow depth of field, heavy motion blur on moves.
- **When to choose it:** brands with a strong single accent colour and a premium/tech feel. If the brand's own world is light (WhatsApp, epara), prefer `light-ui-stroke` / light stages.
