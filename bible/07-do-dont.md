# 07 — Lessons & Watch-outs

> Lessons from references and user feedback. These are defaults with reasons, not laws:
> break one when the idea calls for it, and say why. Format: `- DO/DON'T: lesson — why [ref or feedback date]`
- DON'T: put a light brand on a dark keynote stage — it reads as "any tech ad". [ref: 2026-11-15-keemfinity-whatsapp-specad]
- DON'T: use neon bloom / lens streaks on a light stage — they look like stickers. Use crisp strokes + contact shadow + spill. [ref: whatsapp]
- DON'T: show two light heads at once, even for 0.1 s. [ref: whatsapp]
- DON'T: time-stretch the voice. Move the picture. [ref: both]
- DON'T: stack 8-bit frames for motion blur (ghosts, lavender cast). Average in Float32. [ref: moniepoint]
- DON'T: exit type by sinking or slicing; don't let rising letter tips show as dots. [ref: moniepoint]
- DON'T: use a white flash on impacts. [ref: whatsapp]
- DON'T: redraw, recolour or distort a brand logo. [ref: both]
- DO: open on something only the brand owns. [ref: both]
- DO: approve the hardest 8 s as a look test before building the rest. [ref: moniepoint]
- DO: read a 0.25 s contact sheet like a client before every long render. [ref: both]
- DO: put the logo on a beat after a silence. [ref: moniepoint]
- DO: ask early how the brand name is pronounced before generating any voice-over; TTS often gets the stress wrong in less-common languages (epara = e-pa-RÁ). Fallback: keep the brand on screen only. [feedback 2026-11-16]
- DO: let the logo's own idea be the story (Amazon smile = arrow a→z → one line that does every job). [ref: 2026-11-16-amazon-a-to-z]
- DO: make frame 0 already moving; land the hook slam inside the first ~0.2 s. [ref: amazon-a-to-z]
- DO: make 9:16 a real re-layout: bigger phone, tighter groups, type re-wrapped; never a crop. [ref: amazon-a-to-z, starter-kit]
- DO: make objects real: bevelled edges, tape, soft dents, a shadow that grows as things land; real UI rows, not skeleton bars. [ref: amazon-a-to-z]
- DON'T: put anything but the hero hit within 60 ms of the hero hit. [ref: amazon-a-to-z]
- DON'T: speed up the voice-over; record at natural speed and move the picture. [ref: amazon-a-to-z, keemfinity]
- NOTE: dark vs light stage depends on the brand: WhatsApp needed light, Amazon works dark with bright heroes. [ref: whatsapp, amazon-a-to-z]
